## How to read the work

- **Established intent** comes from the founder's statements in this conversation.
- **Documented finding** comes from a linked source. A provider's procedure describes that provider; a state example describes that jurisdiction.
- **Hypothesis** is a possible interpretation that still needs investigation.
- **Open decision** has not been settled by the founder or validated with users.

The research focuses on U.S. processes as a working assumption. Geography has not been confirmed. It includes published research and public institutional procedures, not interviews conducted for this product or completed claims/account-recovery trials.
