# Evidence register

Research assembled October 29, 2026. “Reviewed” means the page, abstract, form, or policy text was opened and read in this research session by the primary researcher or a delegated researcher. It does not mean a live transaction, authenticated workflow, legal review, or user trial was completed.

Official sources establish their own procedures. Competitor pages establish documented offerings or marketing claims, not effectiveness. Search-result snippets alone are not counted as verified policy. Some source pages were updated recently; others are historical and explicitly identified.

## User problem and practical work

### R01

[Crooks et al., private-sector interactions following bereavement, 2024 — PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39181702/). Reviewed abstract: UK qualitative secondary analysis of 1,133 survey respondents; administrative burden, difficult interactions, paperwork, and timelines. Full-text access was blocked. Do not generalize its sample into U.S. prevalence or product demand.

### R02

[Memorial Sloan Kettering: What To Do After a Loved One Dies](https://www.mskcc.org/cancer-care/patient-education/what-to-do-after-loved-one-dies). Reviewed; updated March 4, 2026. Practical death-confirmation, arrangements, documentation, notifications, records, and support guidance. Some medical/portal details are institution-specific.

### R03

[Iowa Attorney General: Steps Following the Death of a Loved One](https://www.iowaattorneygeneral.gov/for-consumers/general-consumer-information/life-events/death-of-a-loved-one). Reviewed. Property protection, working records, inventory, roles, documents and notifications. State guidance; do not universalize Iowa law.

### R04

[FTC: Shopping for Funeral Services](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/shopping-funeral-services). Reviewed; historical consumer guidance. Written pricing and separate purchasing choices. It is not a complete current state disposition-law reference.

## Insurance, benefits, finances, and authority

### R05

[MetLife: File a Claim](https://www.metlife.com/support-and-manage/file-a-claim/). Reviewed group-life FAQs. Employer contact initiates the employer-sponsored route; beneficiary statements, death documentation, and representative-specific evidence follow. This is MetLife's procedure, not every insurer's.

### R06

[Pacific Life: Employer/Group Policyholder Life Claim Package](https://www.pacificlife.com/content/dam/paclife/wbd/public/Life-Employer-Claim-Package.pdf). Reviewed actual packet. Separates employer coverage/beneficiary verification, each beneficiary's statement, representative evidence, and accidental-death documentation. Does not guarantee eligibility or payment.

### R07

[Guardian: Life and AD&D claim documentation checklist](https://guardianlife.custhelp.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/85). Reviewed by delegated researcher. Describes claimant/employer completion and additional accident-related evidence; not a universal definition of covered accidental death.

### R08

[NAIC: Life Insurance Policy Locator](https://content.naic.org/article/naic-life-insurance-policy-locator-helps-consumers-find-lost-life-insurance-benefits). Reviewed; 2022 official explanation. Participating insurers can contact an eligible beneficiary if a match is found. The service does not give the requester a guaranteed complete inventory or a response in all cases.

### R09

[CMS: COBRA Questions and Answers](https://www.cms.gov/CCIIO/Programs-and-Initiatives/Other-Insurance-Protections/cobra_qna). Reviewed. Describes qualifying events, covered beneficiaries, notices, elections and continuation; explicitly distinguishes public-sector administration and other applicable regimes.

### R10

[26 CFR §54.4980B-6: election rules](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-54/section-54.4980B-6), [29 CFR §2590.606-4: election notices](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-XXV/subchapter-L/part-2590/subpart-A/section-2590.606-4). Current regulatory text reviewed by delegated researcher. Timing has specific triggers and scope. Filing, notice, election, and payment clocks are different.

### R11

[Bank of America: Estate Services](https://www.bankofamerica.com/signature-services/estate-services/). Reviewed. Notification, account review, ownership/beneficiary differences, and legal-document requirements. Account protections can affect existing payment/access arrangements. Provider-specific.

### R12

[USAGov: Agencies to notify when someone dies](https://www.usa.gov/report-a-death). Reviewed; page dated August 31, 2026. Government and financial notifications; SSA/Medicare reporting relationships. Broad navigation guidance, not a personalized eligibility determination.

### R13

[SSA: Form SSA-10 requirements](https://www.ssa.gov/forms/ssa-10.html). Reviewed. Survivor application route, possible documents, original/copy distinctions, and instruction not to delay filing solely because documents are missing. Exact eligibility remains separate.

## Digital accounts

### R14

[Google: Request regarding a deceased user's account](https://support.google.com/accounts/troubleshooter/6357590). Reviewed. Content, funds and closure requests are distinct; passwords are not provided; closure prevents a later content request.

### R15

[Google: Inactive Account Manager](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3036546?hl=en). Reviewed. Advance selected-data sharing, contacts, inactivity trigger and recipient verification. Not a general immediate emergency-access mechanism.

### R16

[Apple: Add a Legacy Contact](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102631), [request access](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102678). Reviewed. Access key and death documentation, scope exclusions, and regional differences. Legacy access is not unrestricted account inheritance.

### R17

[Microsoft: OneDrive digital legacy](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/onedrive/preserve-your-digital-legacy-with-onedrive), [deceased-user account requests](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/manage/accessing-outlook-com-onedrive-and-other-microsoft-services-when-someone-has-died). Reviewed. Advance read-only OneDrive route is distinct from other personal-account/content requests. Legal-request submission does not guarantee release.

### R18

[Meta: online identity after death, 2017](https://about.fb.com/news/2017/08/what-should-happen-to-online-identity/), [memorialization update, 2019](https://about.fb.com/news/2019/04/updates-to-memorialization/). Historical official evidence reviewed for limited legacy management and archive permissions. Current detailed Facebook help was blocked. Current menus, required evidence and full permissions are not verified.

### R19

[X: deceased family member's account](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/contact-x-about-a-deceased-family-members-account), [LinkedIn: deceased member](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1380121/deceased-linkedin-member?lang=en), [LinkedIn: memorialize or close](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1336663/memorialize-or-close-the-account-of-a-deceased-member). Reviewed. Different permitted outcomes and evidence; both platforms restrict survivor login. LinkedIn documents locks and third-party connection termination.

### R20

[LinkedIn: Page admin roles](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a541981). Reviewed by delegated researcher. Organization Page roles are separate from personal profiles. Ordinary admin instructions do not guarantee recovery after a sole admin dies.

### R21

[GoDaddy: access after account holder's death](https://www.godaddy.com/help/how-to-gain-access-to-domains-or-accounts-after-account-holders-death-8356), [domain account transfer](https://www.godaddy.com/help/transfer-my-domain-to-another-godaddy-account-822). Reviewed. Estate-administrator evidence and review; connected website/email products do not move with a domain. Access and transfer are not automatic.

### R22

[CFPB: responsibility for a spouse's debts](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/am-i-responsible-for-my-spouses-debts-after-they-die-en-1467/). Reviewed. Estate versus personal liability, joint obligations and state-law exceptions. Not a determination of a specific person's debt responsibility.

### R23

[Bitwarden: emergency contact setup](https://bitwarden.com/help/add-and-manage-trusted-emergency-contacts/), [emergency access](https://bitwarden.com/help/emergency-access/), [1Password: Emergency Kit](https://support.1password.com/emergency-kit/), [family recovery](https://support.1password.com/recovery/). Reviewed by delegated researcher. Different preparation, acceptance, request, delay, and recovery dependencies. Documented mechanisms do not establish reliability or user success rates.

### R24

[IRS: Retirement topics — Death](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-death). Reviewed. Survivor contact with employer/administrator and beneficiary/plan-dependent payment. Distribution and tax choices require plan-specific investigation.

### R25

[VA: Burial allowance and transportation benefits](https://www.va.gov/burials-memorials/veterans-burial-allowance/). Reviewed. Conditional benefits, claimant/document requirements, and distinct application processes. Not universal veteran/survivor entitlement.

### R26

[USPS: Mail Addressed to the Deceased](https://www.usps.com/manage/mail-for-deceased.htm). Reviewed by delegated researcher. Distinguishes co-resident mail handling from full redirection by appointed representative. Death certificate alone is insufficient for the latter.

### R27

[Orange County Superior Court: Probate Guardianship](https://www.occourts.org/self-help/self-help-probate/probate-guardianship). Reviewed by delegated researcher. California example: nomination differs from court appointment; care and inherited-property guardianship differ. Other parents' rights and existing proceedings matter.

### R28

[Orange County Superior Court: Administering the Probate Estate](https://www.occourts.org/self-help/self-help-probate/wills-trusts/administering-probate-estate). Reviewed by delegated researcher. Appointment, property, inventory, debts/tax and distribution. This does not establish that every asset/death requires formal probate or that California deadlines apply elsewhere.

### R29

[City of Raleigh: Manage Your Utility Account](https://raleighnc.gov/water-and-sewer/services/manage-your-utility-account). Reviewed by delegated researcher. Deceased-holder continuity requires that provider's account process. Local example, not a national utility rule.

## Existing products — documented offerings, not tested outcomes

### R30

[Empathy: bereavement care](https://www.empathy.com/platform/bereavement-care), [estate planning](https://www.empathy.com/platform/estate-planning), [leave support](https://www.empathy.com/platform/leave-support). Official pages reviewed by delegated researcher; bereavement page also reviewed directly. Preparation, personalized plans, care teams, and leave support overlap this concept. Completeness of coverage and actual effectiveness were not tested.

### R31

[Everplans: sharing and Deputies](https://help.everplans.com/hc/en-us/articles/215665558-How-does-sharing-work-on-Everplans-What-is-a-Deputy), [benefits and pensions organization](https://www.everplans.com/articles/how-to-organize-all-your-benefits-and-pensions-information). Reviewed by delegated researcher. Granular sharing and employer-benefit organization are documented. Does not establish automated claims execution.

### R32

[Everplans: unlocking after-death information](https://help.everplans.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000708666-How-do-I-unlock-someone-s-after-death-sections). Reviewed by delegated researcher. Designated unlocker and owner-challenge delay; this is an access mechanism, not legal authority over external accounts.

### R33

[Trustworthy: collaborator access](https://help.trustworthy.com/en/articles/12394561), [employment category](https://help.trustworthy.com/en/articles/13114625). Reviewed by delegated researcher; pages indicate October/August 2026 edits. Living versus legacy roles and benefit/claim information organization are documented. General emergency-access marketing should not be equated with death-verified legacy access.

### R34

[GoodTrust: Digital Vault](https://mygoodtrust.com/digital-vault). Reviewed by delegated researcher. Financial/digital records, insurance, wishes, and Trusted Contacts are documented. Broad emergency language does not establish a specific incapacity-release procedure.

### R35

[When: Platform](https://www.forwhen.com/platform). Reviewed by delegated researcher. Adjacent employer-distributed healthcare-transition services. Does not establish a complete family emergency/death product.

## Coverage limits

- U.S. geography is provisional. No complete state-by-state analysis has been performed.
- Public guidance and forms were reviewed; no actual claim or account request was submitted.
- No original interviews or usability studies have been conducted for this product.
- Current Instagram/TikTok details and detailed current Facebook procedures remain unresolved.
- Provider instructions, actual coverage records, legal authority, and beneficiary entitlement must remain distinct.
- This register supports discovery; it does not validate an MVP, market demand, competitive advantage, or an access/security architecture.
