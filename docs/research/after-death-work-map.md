# Work following a death — research map

Date: October 29, 2026. Scope: U.S. procedural research, with explicit provider and state examples. This map identifies work to investigate and support; it is not a universal checklist or a fixed legal timetable.

## Reading the sequence

The groups below describe dependencies and practical urgency, not mandatory “day 1 / day 7” deadlines. Work can overlap. Death date, coverage-loss date, notice date, claim submission, and court appointment can start different clocks. Relevant institutions and jurisdiction determine actual requirements.

“Possible actor” does not establish that person has authority. Reporting a death, obtaining information, signing a claim, and receiving money are separate acts.

| D05 | Secure property and important records. | Appropriate family helper or estate representative. | Home, vehicles, mail, identification, devices, financial/legal records. | Property and records safeguarded without unauthorized disposal or access. | [R03](evidence-register.md#r03), Iowa guidance. |

## C. Employer, insurance, and survivor benefits

| ID | Work | Possible actor | Information or prerequisite | What establishes progress | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D12 | Contact employer/union benefits administration. | Family contact or beneficiary; employer completes its own obligations. | Employee identity; employer contact; employment/leave/retirement status. | Appropriate administrator and claim process identified. | [R05](evidence-register.md#r05). MetLife's group-life route starts with the employer. |
| D13 | Verify actual basic/supplemental life and accidental-death coverage. | Employer and insurer. | Enrollment, coverage dates, premiums, active-work status, beneficiary records. | Coverage evidence obtained; unresolved terms identified for insurer review. | [R06](evidence-register.md#r06). An offered benefit is not proof of individual coverage. |
| D14 | Complete beneficiary claims. | Each beneficiary or appropriate representative. | Claim statement, death documentation, claimant identity/tax details; additional estate/trust/minor documents if applicable. | Submission and receipt recorded; insurer's outstanding requests tracked. | [R05, R06](evidence-register.md#r05). Reporting the death does not complete every beneficiary's claim. |
| D15 | Investigate accidental-death claims when relevant. | Claimant, employer, insurer; reports supplied by relevant authorities. | Applicable coverage and circumstances; requested accident, police, medical-examiner or related records. | Required evidence supplied or pending records identified. | [R06, R07](evidence-register.md#r06). Sudden death does not itself establish AD&D eligibility. |
| D16 | Find private or unknown life/annuity policies. | Potential beneficiary or appropriate requester. | Known policy details; for a locator request, required deceased/requester information. | Insurer identified or search submitted; outcome understood within locator limits. | [R08](evidence-register.md#r08). NAIC does not promise a complete inventory or a response to every requester. |
| D17 | Determine survivors' continuing health, dental, and vision arrangements. | Covered survivors and benefits administrator. | Current enrollment, coverage-end date, applicable continuation rules, notices and costs. | Available options and actual election/payment deadlines established. | [R09, R10](evidence-register.md#r09). Eligibility and employer/plan scope matter. |
| D18 | Complete each applicable continuation election and payment. | Qualified beneficiary or permitted representative. | Election notice, selected coverage, initial premium and subsequent due dates. | Election and payment evidence retained; coverage status confirmed. | [R09, R10](evidence-register.md#r09). Election and premium payment are separate actions. |
| D19 | Confirm SSA notification and investigate survivor benefits. | Funeral provider may report; eligible survivor applies as appropriate. | Death/identity/relationship information and SSA's requested evidence. | Reporting confirmed; relevant survivor application initiated or existing-benefit handling clarified. | [R12, R13](evidence-register.md#r12). SSA says not to delay filing solely for missing documents. |
| D20 | Investigate retirement and other applicable survivor benefits. | Beneficiary and plan administrator; appropriate agency if veteran/federal/other special coverage applies. | Plan records, relationship/beneficiary evidence, service records when relevant. | Each relevant plan or agency contacted and its claim requirements established. | [R24, R25](evidence-register.md#r24). These benefits have separate eligibility and application processes. |
| D21 | Resolve existing disability claims or possible unpaid benefits. | Insurer, employer, and appropriate claimant/representative. | Actual contract, existing claim status and any survivor-benefit provisions. | Plan-specific instructions obtained. | Research gap: the label “LTD” does not establish a death benefit or how an existing claim is settled. |

| D24 | Maintain necessary household services and change billing/ownership appropriately. | Responsible occupant or estate/trust representative. | Provider, account/service address, occupancy and authority. | Provider's continuity process completed or scheduled. | [R29](evidence-register.md#r29), Raleigh Water example only; other provider rules need verification. |

| D26 | Complete required estate administration and tax work. | Appropriate appointed representative and tax/estate professionals. | Asset/ownership records, valuation and tax records, appointment documents. | Applicable inventory, filings, liabilities, and distribution process resolved. | [R28, R36](evidence-register.md#r28). California example deadlines are not national deadlines. |

## What an advance plan could supply — hypothesis, not approved scope

The map suggests investigating the value of an institution inventory, actual coverage records, document locations, current contacts, legally established roles, platform legacy settings, personal wishes, and a usable recipient handoff. It does not establish that every item should be stored in this app or that the app should execute the tasks.

## Research still needed

- Jurisdiction-specific authority, disposition, probate, creditor, tax, property, and guardianship rules.
- Users with unmarried partners, blended families, disputed beneficiaries, minor beneficiaries, no will, or no appointed representative.
- Death abroad, accidental/investigated death, military benefits, self-employment, and business continuity.
- Other dependents, pet care, housing/tenancy, home/vehicle insurance, and provider-specific service continuity.
- Observed case histories establishing which tasks families actually encounter, miss, repeat, delegate, or find hardest.
- A separate equivalent map for living incapacity and recovery after an emergency.
